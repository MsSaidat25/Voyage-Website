Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last updated: April 2025

1. Information We Collect

When you use our contact form or communicate with us, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Travel preferences and trip details you share with us
  • Your subscription preference for our travel specials newsletter

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your travel enquiries and plan your trips
  • Send you travel specials and promotions if you have opted in
  • Improve our services and website experience

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Form submissions are processed via Netlify, whose privacy policy governs the handling of form data on their platform. Our AI chat assistant is powered by OpenRouter. No chat conversations are stored on our servers.

4. Cookies

Our website stores a single preference cookie (vv-theme) in your browser to remember your light or dark mode preference. This cookie contains no personal data and is not used for tracking.

5. Third-Party Services

We use Google Fonts to serve typefaces. Google may collect limited usage data per their privacy policy. We do not use advertising networks or third-party analytics on this website.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at hello@voyagevista.ca.

7. Unsubscribing

If you have opted in to receive travel specials and wish to unsubscribe, please email us at hello@voyagevista.ca and we will remove you promptly.
